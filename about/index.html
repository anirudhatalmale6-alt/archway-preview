About

“At Archway Hospitality Group, we bridge the gap between rigorous craftsmanship and effortless experience. We are architects of culinary heritage, turning raw elements into enduring memories, from the integrity of our kitchens to the plate at our tables.”

Vision

A Cornerstone of Culinary Identity

To be the cornerstone of the New York culinary identity, recognized as a standard-bearer for quality, innovation, and long-term craftsmanship in both the retail marketplace and the dining room.

Mission

Kitchen to Guest

Our mission is to create a seamless connection between the kitchens and the guests. We are committed to the meticulous production of artisanal food products and the operation of exceptional dining experiences, guided by an unwavering dedication to professional discipline, regional culinary traditions, and the lasting relationships we build within our community.

Restaurant profiles

Distinct by Design

Two Albany dining experiences shaped by their own culinary traditions.

J.C. Hwang’s Albany

Located at 30 South Pearl Street / 45 Beaver Street, J.C. Hwang’s Albany brings the rich heritage of imperial classic Sichuan cuisine to the table. Centuries-old culinary traditions meet modern hospitality in an exceptional dining experience for every time of day.

  • All-Day Dining: Curated all-you-can-eat menus for breakfast, lunch, and dinner
  • Sichuan Heritage: Authentic, masterfully balanced flavors inspired by imperial classics
  • Chef’s Table: Exclusive fine dining through the 9-Bowl Tasting Experience
Visit the restaurant View menu

Robata Albany

Located on the patio at 30 South Pearl Street / 45 Beaver Street, Robata Albany brings an authentic Japanese robatayaki experience into a vibrant outdoor setting. Charcoal-grill cooking meets a lively, social atmosphere made for gathering with friends and family.

  • Interactive Grilling: Every table features a self-serve outdoor grill
  • Massive Selection: More than 80 meats, seafood, vegetables, sides, and desserts
  • All-You-Can-Eat: Grill and enjoy as much as you like at your own pace
  • Outdoor Atmosphere: Designed for friends and families to share an evening together
