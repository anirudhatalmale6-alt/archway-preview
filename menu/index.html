Also known as the Dam Banquet (Ba Ba Yan), the "Nine Big Bowls" (Jiu Da Wan) is a traditional communal feast deeply rooted in Sichuan’s rural culture. It originated from historic "Field Banquets," where villagers would gather to cook a whole pig in nine distinct ways, ensuring every part of the animal was utilized to feed the community.

While there is no single "official" menu—as recipes vary by region and family tradition—the feast is defined by the "Three Steams and Nine Knocks" (San Zheng Jiu Kou) technique. Dishes are primarily steamed, ensuring melt-in-your-mouth tenderness and maximum efficiency when cooking for large gatherings.

Why Nine Bowls?

In Sichuan culture, the number nine (Jiu) is a homophone for "long-lasting" (Jiu), symbolizing longevity, eternal friendship, and prosperity for both the hosts and their guests.

The Traditional 9-Bowl Heritage Menu

While individual dishes may evolve, a classic "Nine Big Bowls" banquet traditionally features these nine iconic items:

  1. The Head Bowl香碗Xiang Wan

    A savory meat cake topped with a golden egg skin. As the "etiquette bowl," it officially signals the start of the feast.

  2. Sweet Pork Belly夹沙肉Jia Sha Rou

    Sweet steamed pork belly stuffed with red bean paste and sticky rice. The fat is steamed until it becomes translucent ("glass pork").

  3. Savory Pork Belly咸烧白Xian Shao Bai

    Savory pork belly steamed over a bed of Ya Cai (Sichuan pickled mustard greens), resulting in a rich, melt-in-your-mouth texture.

  4. Crispy Pork Soup酥肉Su Rou

    Deep-fried, battered pork pieces that are subsequently steamed in a rich, flavorful broth until they absorb the soup.

  5. Steamed Beef粉蒸牛肉Fen Zheng Niu Rou

    Tender beef coated in toasted, spiced rice powder and steamed, traditionally served over a layer of sweet potatoes.

  6. Whole Fish清蒸全鱼Qing Zheng Yu

    Serves as a vital symbol of prosperity (Yu, which sounds like "surplus"), presented whole with the head and tail intact.

  7. Steamed Chicken扣鸡Kou Ji

    A gesture of high respect for guests; chicken is meticulously steamed to lock in its pure, natural juices.

  8. Crispy Ribs蒸酥排骨Su Pai Gu

    Battered and fried pork ribs, then steamed until the meat effortlessly falls off the bone.

  9. Eight Treasure Rice八宝饭Ba Bao Fan

    The sweet finale: sticky rice steamed with red sugar and an assortment of dried fruits and nuts, flipped tableside to reveal a vibrant display.
