Career Opportunity

Beer and Beverage Promoter

We are seeking a charismatic and outgoing Beer and Beverage Promoter to drive drink sales, explain pairings, lead promotions, and elevate the guest experience.

← All open positions

Key Responsibilities

  • Recommend premium beers, signature cocktails, wines, and daily specials to guests.
  • Lead themed beverage nights, happy hours, brewery takeovers, and limited-time activations.
  • Explain flavor profiles, production methods, origins, and culinary pairings.
  • Set up beverage displays, promotional materials, and branded merchandise.
  • Represent featured brewery and distillery partners accurately.
  • Track sales, gather guest feedback, and report beverage trends.
  • Verify IDs and monitor responsible alcohol service.

Skills and Qualifications

  • Strong interpersonal, persuasion, storytelling, and upselling skills.
  • Knowledge of craft beer, wine, and cocktail trends.
  • Confidence approaching guests and speaking to small groups.
  • Ability to collaborate with bartenders and servers without disrupting service.
  • Must meet the legal age requirement for alcohol service.
  • TIPS, ServSafe Alcohol, or equivalent certification is required.
  • Hospitality sales, brand ambassador, promotional, or bartending experience is strongly preferred.

Work Environment

  • Vibrant, high-volume restaurant and bar.
  • Weekend, evening, and holiday availability when guest traffic is highest.
