Career Opportunity

Runner

We are seeking a fast, energetic, and observant Food Runner to organize finished plates, carry trays, and deliver dishes accurately and professionally.

← All open positions

Key Responsibilities

  • Transport plates from the kitchen pass to the correct tables without delay.
  • Deliver dishes by seat number without auctioning food at the table.
  • Verify modifications, sides, and allergy restrictions against kitchen tickets.
  • Protect proper hot and cold serving temperatures.
  • Announce dishes and provide final table-side accompaniments.
  • Assist guests with immediate condiment, refill, or utensil needs.
  • Restock and organize the expo line and support service-station upkeep.

Skills and Qualifications

  • Ability to carry loaded trays up to 30 pounds through a busy dining room.
  • Stamina to walk quickly and climb stairs for 6–8 hour shifts.
  • Visual knowledge of menu items, ingredients, and garnishes.
  • Calm composure during intense service and strong teamwork.
  • Entry-level candidates are welcome; this role provides a foundation for becoming a server.

Work Environment

  • High-motion work between a hot kitchen and busy dining room.
  • Open availability for weekend nights, holidays, and prime dining hours.
